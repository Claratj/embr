`Button` gains `inverse` and `inverse-solid` variants for the inverse surfaces (they switch their focus ring to `focus.ringOnInverse`), an `lg` size for hero and closing calls, and `iconLeft` / `iconRight` slots that render decorative, `aria-hidden` glyphs sized to the label. Icons also work with `asChild`: they land inside the child element. `ButtonSize` is now exported.
